# vetura — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 17 September 2026

**Contact:** privacy@vetura.mk

vetura is a marketplace for used cars in North Macedonia. Sellers pay per listing; buyers
search and message sellers. We never handle the car, the money paid for a car, or any
paperwork — we connect the two people and stop there.

This policy says exactly what we hold, why, and how to get rid of it.

## What we collect

**Because you have an account**
- Your phone number. It is your login; we send a one-time code to it to prove it is yours.
- A display name, if you set one, and a business name if you sell as a dealer.
- Your country and town, your chosen language, and whether you sell privately or as a dealer.
- The date the account was created.

**Because you use the app**
- Listings you create: the car's details, your description, the town, and the photographs you
  upload. Photographs are re-encoded on our server, which strips embedded metadata including
  any location the camera recorded.
- Messages you send to other users, and when they were read.
- Cars you save, searches you save, and reports you send us about a listing.
- Your credit balance and the ledger behind it: every purchase and every listing it paid for.

**Because of how the service runs**
- A push notification token for each device you allow notifications on.
- Request logs, including IP address, used to apply rate limits and to find faults. Kept 30 days.

We do **not** collect your device's location, your contacts, your calendar, advertising
identifiers, or anything for advertising or profiling. There is no analytics SDK and no
advertising SDK in the app.

## Why we hold it, and on what basis

| What | Why | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Phone number, one-time codes | To let you sign in and to keep accounts unique | Performance of a contract |
| Profile, listings, photographs | To publish what you are selling | Performance of a contract |
| Messages | To let buyers and sellers talk | Performance of a contract |
| Credits and purchase records | To sell listing credits and keep an auditable ledger | Contract; legal obligation for records |
| Push tokens | To tell you about replies, matches and expiring listings | Consent (the system prompt) |
| Request logs, rate limits | To keep the service up and stop abuse | Legitimate interest |
| Reports about listings | To act on scams and misuse | Legitimate interest |

## Who else sees it

- **Other users.** Anything on a published listing is public, including your display name,
  your town, and the photographs. Your phone number is visible to a signed-in user on a
  published listing, because that is how a buyer calls you about the car.
- **Apple and Google** process purchases of credits. We never see your card.
- **RevenueCat** tells our server that a purchase completed, so credits can be granted.
- **Meta (WhatsApp Cloud API)** delivers the one-time sign-in code to your number.
- **Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging** deliver notifications.
- **Our hosting and object storage providers** store the database and the photographs.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

## How long we keep it

- Your account and everything attached to it: until you delete it.
- Deleted listings: removed from search immediately; the row is kept while the account exists
  so the conversations about it still make sense, and goes when the account goes.
- Request logs: 30 days.
- Purchase records: seven years, because tax law requires it. These are transaction records —
  amount, date, product — not your profile.

## Deleting your account

**Profile → Delete my account**, two taps from anywhere in the app. It is immediate and it is
not reversible. It deletes your profile, your listings and their photographs, your messages,
your saved cars and searches, your device tokens, and your credit balance — including any
credits you have not spent, which are not refunded. Only the purchase records the law makes
us keep survive it.

You can also write to privacy@vetura.mk from the number on the account.

## Your rights

Under the Macedonian Law on Personal Data Protection and, where it applies, the GDPR, you can
ask for a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how it is used, and
receive it in a portable form. Write to privacy@vetura.mk. We answer within 30 days. If you
are not satisfied you can complain to the Agency for Personal Data Protection of the Republic
of North Macedonia (dzlp.mk).

## Children

vetura is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly hold data about children. If you
believe a child has an account, write to privacy@vetura.mk and we will remove it.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially we will say so in the app before the change takes effect.
The date at the top always reflects the current version.
